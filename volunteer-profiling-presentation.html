Volunteer Profiling

Points & Weightage — Seeing the Full Picture of Involvement

Why giving every touchpoint a score transforms scattered data into a clear, actionable ranking of volunteer engagement — and why having it all in one place is what makes it work.

📍 Koramangala Center · May 2026
The gap we keep running into
We know roughly who our regulars are — but "roughly" isn't good enough when we need to make real decisions. These three questions come up every planning cycle and we have no clean answer.
🤔

"Who is truly our most involved volunteer?"

Someone attends every satsang but never joins activities. Another does every volunteering shift but rarely attends programs. Who is "more involved"? Without weightage, we can't compare them fairly.

📉

"Who is drifting and needs a nudge?"

We only notice someone has drifted when a coordinator remembers a face missing for a few weeks. There's no systematic way to catch gradual disengagement before it becomes permanent absence.

🏆

"Who should we put forward for the next big program?"

Decisions like "who runs the next program", "who gets a deeper role", or "who do we recognize at the annual event" are made on gut feel. A score-based profile makes it transparent and fair.

A profile is not just a contact record. It is a living score built from every recorded touchpoint — 1 point for every participation, summed across every column in the system.

How the score works — 1 point per participation
Every column in the system — each activity, program, or outreach call — adds 1 point when a participation is recorded. The total across all columns is the volunteer's score.
Column / Touch-point Category Points Each What gets counted
Satsang Setup / Breakdown Volunteering 1 Physical presence + service; happens frequently — counts every session
Event Volunteering (Savesoil, Walkathon…) Volunteering 1 Each event volunteered for adds 1 — multiple events in a year stack up
Satsang Attendance Program 1 Regular practice attendance; frequency builds the score over time
Inner Engineering Program Program 1 Each program completed adds 1 — completing it multiple times counts each time
Darshan / Special Event Program 1 Each darshan or special event attended adds 1 to the program column
Isha Kriya / Short Program Program 1 Entry-level program; counts once (completion, not repeat)
Campaign Call — Completed Outreach 1 Volunteer-initiated contact with feedback recorded; quality interaction
Consistency Bonus (3+ sessions in a month) Bonus +3 Rewards sustained engagement over a rolling 30-day window
Tenure Bonus (every full year active) Bonus +5 Long-term loyalty; year count multiplied by 5 added to lifetime score

* Each column in the contacts table maps directly to one of these rows. The score is just the sum of all column counts for a volunteer.

Volunteer tiers — what the score means
Tiers give the score a human meaning. They communicate depth of involvement at a glance and guide decisions on recognition and responsibility.

50 – 99 pts

🥈

20 – 49 pts
Growing involvement. Consistently attending and occasionally volunteering. Great candidates for deeper nurturing.
🌱
Active
1 – 19 pts
Recently joined or occasionally attending. Every engagement counts. Consistent follow-up keeps them in the fold.

Why having everything in one place is what makes it work
The 1-point system only works when all the data it needs — attendance, campaigns, programs, contacts — flows into the same database automatically. Here's what that single source of truth unlocks.
🗂️

No manual aggregation

Scores are computed from data already being collected for attendance and campaigns. There is no separate "profiling spreadsheet" to maintain — the system calculates it as a by-product of normal operations.

🔗

Every touchpoint linked to a single identity

When a volunteer attends a satsang on Saturday and volunteers for a campaign on Tuesday, both events are recorded under the same contact record — deduplicated by phone number. The score is always complete.

📵

Offline attendance still contributes

Attendance taken offline in the hall syncs automatically when back online. No attendance data is lost, which means no points are lost, which means the profile stays accurate even in poor-connectivity situations.

🔁

New coordinators inherit full context

When a coordinator leaves or a new team joins, the full profile history is already there. No tribal knowledge, no "ask the previous person" — the score tells the story of every volunteer going back years.

🎯

Targeted outreach, not blanket messages

Filter volunteers by tier + area + last-seen date and you get an exact list for a campaign — no guessing, no broadcast-to-everyone. Outreach becomes precise and personal instead of noisy.

📊

Center-level health at a glance

Aggregate scores tell you not just individual involvement but the health of the whole center — how many Platinum volunteers do we have? Is the Silver cohort growing? Are Active volunteers progressing or dropping off?
