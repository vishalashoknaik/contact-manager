What you can do
Six key workflows your center team uses every week.
📊

Know who is engaged

Every contact shows activity counts across sessions. Instantly spot consistent meditators and those who have drifted — filter by area, program, or activity.

✅

Take attendance digitally

Create a session, pick programs and areas, assign takers, and let volunteers mark attendance on their phones. Totals update live. Works offline in the hall.

📣

Run outreach campaigns

Create a campaign, assign volunteers, and track every call — feedback, status, notes — all in one place. No more lost follow-up threads over WhatsApp.

👥

Manage contacts easily

Add contacts one at a time or import via CSV. Update area, program, or activity in bulk across multiple contacts with a single action.

🔑

Assign roles without sharing logins

Grant Attendance Taker or Coordinator access with one click. Each person logs in with their own phone number — no shared credentials.

⚙️

Configure the center your way

Add your own Programs, Areas, and Activities from the Admin panel. Dropdowns across the app update immediately — no code changes needed.

Simple phone + PIN login

Each team member logs in with their phone number and a PIN. The coordinator sets up access from the Admin panel. The initial PIN is the phone number itself — it can be changed later.

  • Phone number is the username; PIN is the password
  • Initial PIN equals phone number — change it after first login
  • Works on any device: desktop, tablet, or mobile browser
  • Role is enforced server-side on every request

Attendance setup — ready in under a minute

Before each session, a coordinator creates an attendance session by naming it and selecting which Programs, Areas, and Activities apply. Previous sessions remain available to continue or review.

  • Sessions are reusable — continue from where you left off
  • Programs and Areas match your center's configuration
  • Assign one or more attendance takers per session
  • Delete old sessions that are no longer needed

Taking attendance — fast and foolproof

Attendance takers open the session on their phone. They type a phone number and the contact's details auto-fill from the database. Each submission is logged instantly.

  • Phone lookup auto-fills name, gender, and area
  • New contacts are added on the fly during the session
  • Live "attended" counter visible at all times
  • Works offline — entries queue locally and sync on reconnection
  • Coordinator can assign or reassign takers at any time

Campaigns — structured outreach

A campaign is an outreach drive targeting a group of contacts. Create one for a special event, a follow-up drive, or re-engaging lapsed meditators. Assign volunteers and track every call.

  • Create campaigns with a name and message template
  • Status badges (Pending, Completed, Skipped) at a glance
  • Each volunteer sees only their assigned contacts
  • Coordinator sees the full picture across all volunteers

Access control — manage your team

  • Coordinator — can manage contacts, campaigns, and sessions
  • Access requests shown for approval before being granted
  • Remove access instantly if someone leaves the team

  • A yellow banner notifies the taker that offline mode is active
  • As soon as connectivity is restored, all queued entries sync automatically
  • No manual re-entry or reconciliation needed
  • Works on mobile browsers — no app installation required
