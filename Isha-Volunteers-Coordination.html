Problems this app solves
Six real pain points our center team was facing every week — here’s what this app addresses.
📊

We couldn’t tell who was still active

The contact list kept growing but we had no way to see who was regularly attending. Now every contact shows their count per activity, area, and program — drift is visible at a glance.

✅

Attendance was always manual — and often lost

If the person who took attendance forgot to enter it, that session was gone. Now volunteers mark attendance on their phones during the session. It works offline in the hall and syncs automatically.

📣

Follow-up calls had no structure

Campaign assignments and call notes were scattered across WhatsApp threads. Now each campaign has volunteers assigned, and every call — status, feedback, notes — is logged in one place.

👥

Managing the contact list was messy

Data was duplicated, stale, or split across spreadsheets. Now contacts are imported via CSV or added one at a time, and bulk updates to area, program, or activity take a single click.

🔑

New members had no proper access setup

We used to share common logins or give everyone full access. Now each volunteer logs in with their own phone number and sees only what their role allows — set up in seconds by the coordinator.

⚙️

Every center has different programs and areas

Hardcoded dropdowns didn’t match our center’s reality. Now programs, areas, and activities are configured from the Admin panel and the whole app updates instantly — no code changes needed.

Simple phone + PIN login

Each team member logs in with their phone number and a PIN. The coordinator sets up access from the Admin panel — each person gets their own credentials.

  • Phone number is the username; PIN is set by the coordinator
  • Works on any device: desktop, tablet, or mobile browser
  • Role is enforced server-side on every request

Attendance setup — ready in under a minute

Before each session, a coordinator creates an attendance session by naming it and selecting which Programs, Areas, and Activities apply. Previous sessions remain available to continue or review.

  • Sessions are reusable — continue from where you left off
  • Programs and Areas match our center's configuration
  • Assign one or more attendance takers per session
  • Delete old sessions that are no longer needed

Taking attendance — fast and foolproof

Attendance takers open the session on their phone. They type a phone number and the contact's details auto-fill from the database. Each submission is logged instantly.

  • Phone lookup auto-fills name, gender, and area
  • New contacts are added on the fly during the session
  • Live "attended" counter visible at all times
  • Works offline — entries queue locally and sync on reconnection
  • Coordinator can assign or reassign takers at any time

Campaigns — structured outreach

A campaign is an outreach drive targeting a group of contacts. Create one for a special event, a follow-up drive, or re-engaging lapsed meditators. Assign volunteers and track every call.

  • Create campaigns with a name and message template
  • Status badges (Pending, Completed, Skipped) at a glance
  • Each volunteer sees only their assigned contacts
  • Coordinator sees the full picture across all volunteers

  • As soon as connectivity is restored, all queued entries sync automatically
  • No manual re-entry or reconciliation needed
  • Works on mobile browsers — no app installation required
